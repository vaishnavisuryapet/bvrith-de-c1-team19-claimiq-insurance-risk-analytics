## 3. Key Decisions

* Power BI dashboards were built using the validated Gold-layer outputs.
* KPI cards were used for important high-level insurance metrics.
* Slicers were included to allow interactive filtering of dashboard results.
* Claim amount, approved amount, claim count, and SLA-related metrics were presented using suitable visualizations.
* Dashboard metrics were cross-checked with Databricks Gold tables to ensure consistency.
* Only relevant business metrics were included to keep the dashboards clear and easy to understand.
